INTERNSHIP SUMMER PROJECT / COPYWRITING & A/B TESTING

A different voice.
The same opportunity.

When every business was hiring, another familiar recruitment message was easy to overlook. Our challenge was to give prospective production employees a reason to stop and pay attention.

The challenge

During the COVID-19 pandemic era, employers were competing for workers in a crowded hiring market. For my Valmont summer internship project, I worked with the marketing team to explore messaging that would stand apart from the company’s usual approach and connect with a blue-collar audience.

My contribution

I wrote the advertising copy, working alongside a marketing team that included a graphic designer who developed the visuals. Headlines such as “You got skills?” and “Make money. And a difference.” paired a conversational tone with the practical benefits and purpose of a career in skilled trades.

Test, learn, present

We used A/B testing to compare these ads with Valmont’s typical messaging. Our findings showed that the new creative resonated more strongly with the production audience we wanted to reach. I presented the project and findings to upper management to conclude my internship.

My role: Copywriting, collaboration on the campaign, A/B testing, and presenting findings to upper management.

Creative credit: Visual design by a graphic designer on the Valmont marketing team. Copy by Amy Nelson.
